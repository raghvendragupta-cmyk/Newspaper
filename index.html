Order Surge Tests CSAT as Automation Steps Up

August saw order volumes touch 4.8M+, with Week 4 peaking at 562K orders (+40% surge). Wait times improved dramatically, though CSAT faces core pressure...

Operational Highlights:

  • Total Orders: 4,857,752 (August peak week hit 562K)
  • Total Sessions: 341,216 chats offered; 171,542 answered by agents (50% level).
  • Efficiency: BOT resolution achieved 53% of total sessions. Average Wait Time (AWT) dropped sharply from 12:20 to 7:53.
  • Contact Ratio: 20% | First Response Time: 0 mins 23 secs.

Insight: While operational productivity and wait times improved sharply, customer satisfaction experienced significant pressure (CSAT dropped from 17% to 10%, with DSAT rising to 7,887). Resolving core drivers remains the top operational target.
